Our Story

Built on Craft,
Grown by Trust

Founded in 2010, Victoria Shoes began as a small workshop driven by skilled craftsmen who believed that quality footwear should be accessible to everyone in Sri Lanka.

Today, from our fully operational factory in Siyabalagaskotuwa, we produce thousands of pairs annually under our own brands — Vmark, Leemax, and Victoria — while also distributing internationally recognized labels across the island.

We operate as a manufacturer, wholesaler, retailer, and importer — a complete footwear ecosystem built for Sri Lanka, by Sri Lankans.
